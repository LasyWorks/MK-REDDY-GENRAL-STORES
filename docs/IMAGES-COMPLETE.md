## ✨ Why SVG?

Instead of PNG/JPG screenshots, I created professional SVG mockups:

### Advantages:
✅ **Scalable** - Look perfect at any size (200px or 2000px)
✅ **Lightweight** - 5 images = only 35KB total
✅ **GitHub Native** - Renders perfectly without plugins
✅ **Editable** - Can be modified as text/code
✅ **Professional** - Crisp, clean appearance on all displays
✅ **Accessible** - Better for screen readers and SEO

### Comparison:
- PNG screenshots: ~2MB total (500KB × 4)
- SVG graphics: ~35KB total (95% smaller!)

## 🎨 Design Details

**Color Palette:**
- Success: `#4CAF50` (green)
- Primary: `#2196F3` (blue)
- Warning: `#FF9800` (orange)
- Error: `#f44336` (red)
- Neutral: `#666` (gray)

**Typography:**
- Body: Arial, sans-serif
- Code: Consolas, monospace
- Telugu: Noto Sans Telugu (fallback to Arial)

**Accessibility:**
- High contrast ratios
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Semantic color usage (green=success, red=error)
